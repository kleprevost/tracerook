Questions

How do I get access?

The beta is invitation-only. Request an invitation or email kyle@tracerook.dev. Invited members receive the download and a Cloud access code.

Am I charged during the beta?

No. Billing isn't active during the private beta. We'll tell invited members before paid plans begin.

Do I need an Anthropic API key?

No. TraceRook Cloud runs Claude on TraceRook's account. You connect with the access code from your invitation.

What do I need?

An Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 26 or later and Claude Code. Installation guide

What is sent to the cloud?

Only a projection of each ambiguous action: categories and signal codes, re-scanned for secrets. Privacy pipeline

Does it work without Cloud?

Yes. Local rules and native review run entirely on your Mac. Cloud adds Claude's contextual judgment.
